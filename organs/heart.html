The four phases

    Pressure–volume loop of the left ventricle

    Each beat traces a loop: fill (bottom), squeeze with the valves shut (right), eject (top), relax (left). Its width is the stroke volume and its area the work of one beat.

    The pulse at the fingertip: a pulse oximeter’s pleth

    ECG, lead II Fingertip pulse (pleth) Dashed: healthy 25-year-old

    Monitors scale the pleth to fill the screen, and so does this one: the perfusion index gives its real size. Each pulse arrives at the finger a little after the R wave, once the ventricle has squeezed and the wave has run down the arm.
